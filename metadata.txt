about=Media Hub 1.5.0 is a light multimedia dock for QGIS 3.44 (Qt5) on Windows, developed by JulienH77. Play local videos and direct streams using optional VLC, select audio and subtitle tracks, use Picture-in-Picture, fullscreen, speed and volume controls, and configurable keyboard shortcuts. Dark mode switches the plugin to a dark appearance; hide or restore the floating player's controls in PiP and fullscreen, also with the H shortcut. Browse radio by country: five French stations including RTL and Franceinfo, and up to five popular stations from the Radio Browser directory in other countries. Assign a country to each custom station, and keep older stations safely categorized during migration. International radio lists require Internet access and may be unavailable behind corporate proxies, while last successful lists are cached. Browse with optional Qt WebEngine or your system browser; keep local notes. Experimental release; media codecs and streams depend on system VLC, network access and providers. No bundled VLC binaries or telemetry. MIT license.
